REMOTE WORK POLICY

Eligible employees may work remotely for up to three days per week with manager approval.

Employees working remotely must remain available during their team's standard working hours unless a different schedule has been approved.

Company confidential information must only be accessed using approved company devices and secure network connections.

Employees are responsible for maintaining an appropriate working environment while working remotely.

Permanent remote work arrangements require approval from both the employee's manager and Human Resources.
