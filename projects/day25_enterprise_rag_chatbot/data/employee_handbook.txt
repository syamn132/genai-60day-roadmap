EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK

Standard working hours are from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM from Monday to Friday.

Employees are expected to follow company policies relating to attendance, leave, information security, remote work, and professional conduct.

Employees should contact Human Resources for clarification when a policy is unclear.

All employees must protect confidential company and customer information.

Company policies may be updated periodically. Employees should refer to the current approved version of a policy when making decisions.
