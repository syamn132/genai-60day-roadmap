IT SECURITY POLICY

All employee passwords must contain at least 12 characters.

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for company systems that support it.

Employees must not share passwords with colleagues or external parties.

Company information must only be stored on approved systems and devices.

Employees should report suspected phishing emails, unusual login activity, or lost company devices to the IT security team as soon as possible.

Access to company systems must be removed when an employee or contractor leaves the organization.
