ANNUAL LEAVE POLICY

Full-time employees receive 24 days of annual leave per calendar year.

Annual leave should normally be requested through the company's leave management system before the planned absence. Employees should obtain manager approval before making travel arrangements.

Employees may carry forward up to 5 unused annual leave days into the next calendar year. Any unused balance above the permitted carry-forward amount expires at the end of the year unless an exception has been approved by HR.

Leave lasting more than 5 consecutive working days requires manager approval.

Employees who join during the year receive annual leave on a prorated basis according to their joining date.
