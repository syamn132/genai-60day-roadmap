CONTRACTOR POLICY

Contract employees are not eligible for paid annual leave under the standard employee leave policy.

Contractors may request unpaid time off. Such requests must be approved by the contractor's reporting manager and should be planned in advance whenever possible.

Contractors are also not eligible for employee leave carry-forward benefits.

Access to company systems is provided only for the duration of the active contract. Access must be removed when the contract ends.

Contractors must comply with company security and confidentiality requirements while accessing company information.
