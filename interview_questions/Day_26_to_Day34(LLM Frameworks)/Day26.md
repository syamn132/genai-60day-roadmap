1. Prompt engineering is the systematic design, testing, and refinement of instructions, context, constraints, and output requirements to make an LLM behave more reliably for a specific task.

2. What is the difference between prompt engineering and prompt patterns?
A. Prompt engineering is the broader process of designing, testing and refining prompts for reliable model behavior Prompt patterns are reusable prompt structures that solve recurring categories of problems, such as few-shot classification, structured extraction, grounding,decomposition and abstention.
