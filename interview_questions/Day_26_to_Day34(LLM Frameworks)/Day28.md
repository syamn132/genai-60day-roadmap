1. What is LangChain?
A. LangChain is an application framework for working with language models through standardized abstractions for models, messages, prompts, tools, retrieval, and orchestration. It allows developers to integrate multiple model providers behind common interfaces and compose them into larger AI workflows.

2. Why not call the provider SDK directly?
A. For simple applications I may use the provider SDK directly. LangChain becomes useful when I want standardized interfaces, provider portability, reusable components, and more complex workflows involving prompts, retrieval, tools, or agents.
