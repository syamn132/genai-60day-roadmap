1. Pretraining
A. Pretraining is the initial large-scale training phase in which a language model learns general language and world patterns from massive datasets, typically through next-token prediction. The model computes loss against the true next token, backpropagates gradients, and updates its parameters repeatedly to create a general-purpose base model.

2. Transfer Learning
A. Transfer learning is the process of reusing knowledge learned by a model on a large general task or dataset and applying that knowledge to a new related task, usually with less task-specific data and compute. Fine-tuning is one common method of transfer learning.
